Trading Psychology
The thing that actually
destroys accounts is you.
Most trading apps teach mechanics. FinoMundo teaches the one thing that separates consistently profitable traders from everyone else — their relationship with loss, discipline, and emotional state. These are not optional settings. They are gates built into every single trade.
🧠
Emotion tagger — before every trade
Before every trade you select your emotional state: Calm, Focused, Anxious, Greedy, or Revenge. Anxious, Greedy, or Revenge trigger a warning and a chance to step back. Every state is logged to reveal patterns in your journal over time.
✅
Pre-trade checklist — 3 questions
Is this your defined setup? Did you check the higher timeframe? Are you in a clear headspace? Three questions before every execution. One "No" triggers a caution. Professional discipline built as a daily habit — not a lecture.
🟢
Cold head indicator
A persistent header indicator showing your current headspace — Clear, Distracted, or Emotional. Tap to update any time. Professionals check their headspace before market open. FinoMundo makes it the first thing you see every session.
🛑
2-loss daily lockout
After two losing trades the terminal locks automatically. No revenge trading. No escalation. Manual override exists — but logs the decision, warns that traders who override have a 3x higher loss rate for the rest of the day, and requires explicit confirmation. Resets at midnight.
📉
Loss Debrief — learn from every mistake
Close a losing trade and an instant debrief shows exactly what the chart was telling you at entry — trend direction, RSI, higher timeframe conflict. References both the Trading Psychology lesson and the Top-down Analysis lesson. No loss goes to waste.
📔
No-trade journal
Log why you chose NOT to trade today — 6 presets including "No valid setup," "High-impact news event — staying out," and "Not in the right headspace." Every entry adds 10 points to your Prop Firm Readiness Score.
📖
"Your relationship with losing defines your entire trading career. The trader who masters losing will outlast everyone else."

BCB Selic Rate (Brazil)
🔴 High
Brazil's benchmark interest rate. Directly affects BRL/USD, B3 stocks, and credit costs across Brazil. One of the highest benchmark rates in the world — essential for any Brazilian trader.

Federal Reserve Rate Decision
🔴 High
The Fed sets the benchmark rate affecting every financial market on earth. Rate hike = stronger dollar = weaker LatAm currencies. When the Fed moves, BCB and Banxico typically follow within one or two meetings.
⚠️ Largest restriction event · Press conference stop-hunt explained
EIA Crude Oil Inventory
🏸 Medium
Critical for Brazilian traders — Petrobras moves with oil prices. Falling inventories = higher oil = stronger BRL. Mexican traders watch this for Pemex and peso impact. Every Wednesday 10:30 AM EST.

FinoMundo exists for all of them. It started in LatAm because that is where the need was most acute and most ignored. It will not end there.

Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026 · finomundo.world

1. Who we are
FinoMundo is a financial literacy application operated as an independent bootstrapped product. Contact: privacy@finomundo.world

2. Information we collect
We collect your email address when you join the waitlist or create an account, your in-app progress and quiz results, and device information for debugging. We do not collect real financial data or payment details directly — all payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play.

3. How we use your information
Your email is used to notify you at launch, send educational content you opt into, and communicate important app updates. Your progress data personalizes your learning journey. We never sell your data to third parties.

4. Data storage
Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security). All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

5. Children's privacy
FinoMundo is designed for ages 8 and up. Users under 13 require parental consent as required by applicable law (COPPA in the US, LGPD in Brazil). Parents may request deletion of their child's data at any time by contacting privacy@finomundo.world.

6. Your rights
You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact privacy@finomundo.world. Users in the EU/EEA have additional rights under GDPR. Brazilian users have rights under LGPD.

7. Third-party services
FinoMundo uses RevenueCat for subscription management, Supabase for data storage, Beehiiv for email, and Twelve Data for market data.

8. Data retention
We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from within the app or by contacting us.

9. Contact
Questions? Email privacy@finomundo.world — we will respond within 5 business days.
