Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Applies to finomundo.world and the FinoMundo mobile app

1. Who we are

FinoMundo is an independent bootstrapped financial literacy application. We are the data controller for personal information collected through our website and mobile app. You can reach us at privacy@finomundo.world.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Email address — when you join the waitlist or create an account
  • Learning progress — quiz scores, completed lessons, XP, FinoCoins, and streak data
  • Paper trading activity — simulated trades, positions, and journal entries (no real money)
  • Device information — OS version, app version, and device type for debugging
  • Language and country preference — set during onboarding

We do not collect real financial data, bank account details, or payment card information. All subscription payments are processed directly by Apple App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies.

3. How we use your information

  • To notify waitlist members at launch and send educational content they opted into
  • To personalize your learning journey based on your goals, country, and progress
  • To power leaderboards (XP and P/L) — display name only, never email
  • To send streak reminders and market-linked push notifications (opt-out available)
  • To improve the app through aggregate usage analytics

We never sell your data to third parties. We never use your data for advertising.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a PostgreSQL database with Row Level Security (RLS) ensuring each user can only access their own data. All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. API keys and secrets are never exposed to the client.

5. Children's privacy

FinoMundo is designed for users aged 8 and up. We take children's privacy seriously:

  • Users under 13 in the United States require verifiable parental consent under COPPA
  • Users under 13 in Brazil require parental consent under LGPD
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent
  • Parents may request access to or deletion of their child's data at any time

To make a request regarding a child's data, contact privacy@finomundo.world.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out — unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in any email

EU/EEA users have additional rights under GDPR. Brazilian users have rights under LGPD. To exercise any right, contact privacy@finomundo.world. We will respond within 5 business days.

7. Third-party services

FinoMundo uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management
  • Beehiiv — email newsletter and waitlist
  • Twelve Data — delayed market data for paper trading
  • Anthropic (Claude) — AI-powered Daily Market Brief feature
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing

8. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law. Waitlist email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify registered users by email of any material changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions about your privacy?
Email us at privacy@finomundo.world — we respond within 5 business days. For account deletion requests, include your registered email address.
